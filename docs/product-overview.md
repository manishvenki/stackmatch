# StackMatch — Product Overview

## Tagline
**Plan smarter. Choose better. Build with confidence.**

## Mission
StackMatch helps technical and non-technical people turn a simple product idea into a structured, understandable, build-ready project plan.

The user describes their idea naturally in plain words without needing to know technical jargon. StackMatch guides them through an intelligent scoping journey to define:

- Product discussion and requirement clarification
- Functional and non-functional requirements
- Feature planning & user stories
- Technology selection and match scoring
- Side-by-side technology comparisons
- Technology benefits and trade-offs
- Production architecture topography
- Phased development roadmap
- Learning resources and documentation
- Executive project blueprint

## Target Personas

1. **Non-Technical Founders / Creators:** Describe business problems in simple language and receive an intelligible breakdown without being overwhelmed by acronyms.
2. **Students & Aspiring Developers:** Learn how modern full-stack systems fit together and why specific databases or runtimes are selected.
3. **Clients & Product Managers:** Prepare clear, unambiguous scoping briefs for developers and software agencies.
4. **Experienced Engineers:** Accelerate initial technical discovery, trade-off matrix documentation, and RFC proposals.
